# Limitations

- **F0 is not a pipeline.** It does not transcribe, diarize, or attribute speakers.
- Prior private research is **not** claimed to have been production-ready.
- Diarization errors cannot be fully repaired by text-only agents.
- Optional video evidence will remain off until licensed backends exist.
- InsightFace and LightASD are **not** available here pending license review.
- Attribution may return `unresolved`. That is success of the policy, not a failure to “guess.”
- Third-party tools (pyannote, WhisperX, Llama, ECAPA) have their own hardware, license, and quality limits.
- This repository is private during F0; “open source” publication is not implied by Apache-2.0 on the empty package.
